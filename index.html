Welcome to Jen Smith's Portfolio

Hello and thanks for visiting!       

I am a technically-adept communications professional with a Master's degree in Professional Communications and more than 20 years of experience developing strategic communications, marketing content, technical documentation, and customer-facing materials across government, higher education, and technology organizations.

Currently I am a Technical Writer with 10 years of experience, translating complex information to user-focused technical and non‑technical audiences. Prior to that I developed a variety of Communications materials primarily for the College of Charleston and the Louisiana State Medical Society.

My work philosophy comes from my Master of Arts in Professional Communications education, where I learned to apply ethos (credibility), pathos (empathy), and logos (logic) across both technical and creative projects. Over the years, I’ve built websites, written blogs, and created marketing materials that are both user-focused and goal-driven.

I rely on these core principles in all my professional writing:

  • Clean, user-friendly visual design
  • Clear and practical language to support content aligned with a specific user goal
